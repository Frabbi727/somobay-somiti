# Glossary — শব্দকোষ

| English | বাংলা | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Somiti (society) | সমিতি | The cooperative society |
| Member | সদস্য | A person holding shares in the somiti |
| Share | শেয়ার | Unit of membership; each share carries a monthly deposit and service charge |
| Share unit | শেয়ার একক | Monthly savings deposit per share |
| Savings deposit | সঞ্চয় আমানত | Money members save each month; it remains the member's (account 2101) |
| Service charge | সার্ভিস চার্জ | Monthly fee per share; income of the somiti |
| Registration fee | ভর্তি ফি | One-time fee per share when shares are added |
| Due / instalment | বকেয়া / কিস্তি | Amount a member must pay for a month |
| Due date, grace days | শেষ তারিখ, অতিরিক্ত দিন | Payment is late after due date + grace days |
| Late fee | বিলম্ব ফি | Charge for paying late, if the rate plan has one |
| Advance | অগ্রিম জমা | Money paid ahead; used automatically for future dues |
| Paid through | পরিশোধিত (যে মাস পর্যন্ত) | The last month fully covered |
| Rate plan | রেট প্ল্যান / হার পরিকল্পনা | The prices from a given month; needs president and secretary |
| Maker / checker | এন্ট্রিদাতা / অনুমোদনকারী | The person who enters / the different person who approves |
| Awaiting approval | অনুমোদনের অপেক্ষায় | Entered, not yet checked; nothing in the books yet |
| Approved | অনুমোদিত | Checked and recorded in the books |
| Rejected | প্রত্যাখ্যাত | Turned down by the checker before approval, with a reason |
| Withdrawn (cancelled by maker) | প্রত্যাহার করা | The person who entered it took it back before approval |
| Reversed | রিভার্স করা | An approved entry undone by a mirror entry, with a reason; nothing is deleted |
| Returned for correction | সংশোধনের জন্য ফেরত | A registration or rate plan sent back to be fixed and submitted again |
| Voucher | ভাউচার | A numbered double-entry record: RV receipt, PV payment, JV journal, CV contra |
| Journal / ledger | জাবেদা / খতিয়ান | The list of vouchers / one account's history |
| Trial balance | রেওয়ামিল | All account balances; debits equal credits |
| Fiscal year | অর্থবছর | 1 July – 30 June |
| Lock a month | মাস লক | No more entries in that month |
| Year-end | বার্ষিক সমাপনী / বছর-শেষ | Closing the year: profit, statutory funds, dividends |
| Reserve fund | সংরক্ষিত তহবিল | At least 15% of net profit (Cooperative Societies Act s.34) |
| Cooperative development fund | সমবায় উন্নয়ন তহবিল | 3% of net profit, payable |
| Dividend | লভ্যাংশ | Profit shared among members by share-months |
| Share-months | শেয়ার-মাস | Shares held in each month of the year, added up |
| Exit | প্রত্যাহার | A member leaving; their savings and advance are settled and paid out |
| Nominee | নমিনি | Who receives a deceased member's settlement |
| Defaulter | খেলাপি | Member with unpaid dues past the due date |
| Books self-check | হিসাবের স্বয়ংক্রিয় যাচাই | Nightly check that the books add up |
| Audit log | অডিট লগ | Record of every change, who and when |
| Error reference | রেফারেন্স | 8-letter code to give support when something fails |
| Bell | ঘণ্টা 🔔 | In-app messages |
